Getting started

Open a Colab notebook and load a CSV
  1. Go to colab.research.google.com (opens in a new tab) and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose New notebook.
  3. Click the folder icon on the left, then the upload icon, and pick your CSV file.
  4. In the first cell, type import pandas as pd then df = pd.read_csv("your-file.csv") then df.head(), and press Shift+Enter.
  5. Files you upload this way disappear when the notebook closes. Keep a copy in your team folder.
Put your slides in your team folder
  1. Open your team folder. The link is on your table card.
  2. Choose New, then Google Slides. Or upload a PowerPoint or PDF file.
  3. Name it "Team N slides", with your team number.
  4. Remember one slide called "AI we used and how we checked it".
Make a GitHub repo public
  1. On GitHub, choose New repository.
  2. Give it a name and choose Public.
  3. Already made it private? Open the repo's Settings, scroll to the bottom, and choose Change visibility.
  4. Never put passwords, keys or personal information in it.
Check that a link works for someone else
  1. Open a private window: Chrome Ctrl+Shift+N, Mac Cmd+Shift+N, or a private tab on a phone.
  2. Paste your link.
  3. If it asks you to sign in or request access, change the sharing to "anyone with the link" and try again.
