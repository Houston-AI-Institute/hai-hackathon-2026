How to submit

Every team gets its own Google Drive folder. You submit by putting your work in it.

  1. Find your team folder. The link is on your table card. Anyone with the link can edit it, so keep it inside your team.
  2. By 9:30 AM, open the "Our project" Doc in the folder and add your team's first names.
  3. By 2:00 PM, put your work in the folder:
    • Slides (required): Google Slides, PowerPoint or PDF. Name the file "Team N slides", with your team number. Include one slide called "AI we used and how we checked it". For example: "We used Gemini Notebook to answer permit questions. We checked 10 answers against the city's pages. 8 were right. For the other 2, it said it didn't know."
    • Code, if you have it: a zip file, a notebook, or a link.
    • A demo link, if you have one.
    • Screenshots.
    Then fill in the "Our project" Doc: your project name, your main track written as "Track N (Name)", the AI you used and how you checked it, and your links.
  4. Check your outside links. Anything outside the folder (GitHub, Colab, Replit, a demo) must open for someone who isn't signed in. Check each link in a private window. Windows: Ctrl+Shift+N (Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+P). Mac: Cmd+Shift+N (Firefox: Cmd+Shift+P). Phone: open a private tab.
  5. Submissions close at 2:00 PM. Don't change your folder after that. Judges look at what's there at 2:00.
  6. Can't get online or into your folder before 2:00? Tell the help desk, or email initiatives@houstonaiinstitute.com with the subject "SUBMISSION Team N", your links and your main track.
  7. By submitting, your team agrees to the rules, including the open-source section.

You'll need to be signed in to Google to add files.

How to share so anyone can open it

Google Slides, Docs or Colab

Click Share. Under "General access", choose Anyone with the link, set to Viewer. Copy the link.

Canva

Click Share, then set access to Anyone with the link can view. Or download it as a PDF and put the PDF in your team folder.

Gemini Notebook

Click Share. If you see Anyone with a link, turn it on and copy the link. If you don't, put screenshots of your notebook answering questions in your team folder instead.

GitHub

Make the repo Public. See Make a GitHub repo public.

Replit

Make the project public, or publish it, and copy the link. Then test the link in a private window.
