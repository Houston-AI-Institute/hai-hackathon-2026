Public data for open tracks

No ready-made challenge? Pick any public data, say where it came from, and show who it helps. These are official sources to start from.

Tracks 1, 2 and 4 (Growth, Drainage, Development)

  • City of Manvel GIS map (opens in a new tab): city limits, ETJ, zoning, parcels, subdivisions, flood zones and drainage districts. Parcel records show owners' names, so leave names out of your project
  • City of Manvel Development Map (opens in a new tab): plat approvals, new commercial building and city projects
  • City of Manvel adopted plans (opens in a new tab): comprehensive plan, land use and thoroughfare plans, and master plans
  • Census Building Permits Survey (opens in a new tab): new housing permits by city and county
  • FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (opens in a new tab): flood zone maps
  • data.census.gov (opens in a new tab): population, housing and commuting by area
  • Texas Open Data Portal (opens in a new tab): state datasets on many topics

Track 6 (Grid Resiliency & Outage Readiness)

  • ERCOT grid information (opens in a new tab): Texas power demand, supply and grid conditions
  • OpenFEMA data sets (opens in a new tab): disaster declarations and assistance
  • NOAA Storm Events Database (opens in a new tab): storms and damage, by county and date

Track 7 (Healthcare Access & Patient Support)

  • CMS Provider Data Catalog (opens in a new tab): hospitals, clinics and care quality
  • HRSA data (opens in a new tab): health centers and areas short of health workers
  • CDC PLACES (opens in a new tab): local health estimates by county and tract
  • MedlinePlus (opens in a new tab): plain-language health information

Track 8 (Water Conservation & Drought Readiness)

  • TWDB Water Use Survey (opens in a new tab): water use by Texas cities and counties
  • US Drought Monitor (opens in a new tab): weekly drought maps
  • USGS Water Data (opens in a new tab): river, lake and groundwater readings
  • NOAA Climate Data Online (opens in a new tab): rainfall and temperature history
