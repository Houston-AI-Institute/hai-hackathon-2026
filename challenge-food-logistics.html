Track 5 (Food Access & Resource Navigation)

Where will food be needed next?

Some coding helps

The problem

Food banks in the Houston area move millions of pounds of food each year to hundreds of pantries across many counties. When a hurricane, a freeze or a wave of job losses hits, need jumps fast, and not always in the usual places. Planners have to decide where to send trucks and mobile pantries before the full numbers come in. Build a tool that helps them see where need is likely to rise next, and where extra food should go.

Who it's for

Food bank staff who plan where food and mobile pantries go each month.

What to build

The minimum is a target, not a rule. Judges score every team with the same five criteria.

Build at minimum

A forecast or early-warning view for Harris County and the counties around it (you choose the area and say why). It shows:

  • which counties are likely to need more food next month, using public signals;
  • how sure the tool is.

Stretch goals

  • Suggest where to send a set number of extra mobile pantry visits, and explain each suggestion in plain language.
  • Plan simple delivery routes from a depot point you pick.
  • A "what if a hurricane hits next week" scenario.
  • Compare your forecast with what actually happened after a past storm (Harvey in 2017, Beryl in 2024).

Start here

  • No code? Pick one month of SNAP cases, put Harris and the counties around it in Google Sheets, and chart how cases changed month by month.
  • New to Python? See open a Colab notebook and load a CSV.

Good to know

About the data
  • Real food bank shipments, routes and pantry stock aren't public. Use SNAP cases as a stand-in for need, and say so on your AI slide.
  • If you make up pantry locations, trucks or stock, label them "made up" everywhere they appear.
  • Small counties have small numbers, and a jump of a few hundred cases can look huge. Show how sure your tool is.
  • SNAP enrollment files are monthly Excel files, one file per month. Plan time to combine them.
  • The online SNAP files start in January 2022. For Harvey (2017), use other signals, like county unemployment.
  • Use only public data. No personal information.
Planning routes
