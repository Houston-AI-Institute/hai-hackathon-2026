Checkpoints to aim for

  • By 10:30: a one-line problem, and who it helps.
  • By 12:00: a plan for the smallest version that works.
  • By 1:00: one feature working.
  • By 1:45: your slides in your team folder.
  • At 2:00: done.

You don't need to code

Good teams also need people who know the problem, can design, and can present.

Look for the volunteers in white T-shirts. They can help with anything on the day.

We take photos and record the presentations. If you'd rather not be in them, tell us at check-in.

Parents and guardians

  • Sign your student in at the desk at 8:30, and pick them up there at 4:30.
  • A parent or guardian must agree to the open-source rule (every project is shared as open source). Reply to our email, or sign at the desk. Without it, the student can't take part.
  • The offshore incident reports challenge and photos are separate yes or no answers.
  • The on-site phone number is in our email to you.
