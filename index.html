How it works

Step 1 Pick a challenge

A track is a topic. Tracks 3, 5 and 9 have ready-made challenges: a real problem with data ready to use. On the other tracks, you pick the problem and find public data yourself.

You can work on more than one track, but when you submit, pick one main track to be judged on.

Nobody on your team codes? Try the Manvel challenge (Track 3) with Gemini Notebook.

Step 2 Get the data

Every challenge's data, and public data for the open tracks, is on one page. Always say where your data came from.

Go to the data

Step 3 Build with free AI tools

You don't need to code. A no-code tool or a clickable mockup counts. Check what the AI gives you.

See the free AI tools

Step 4 Submit by 2:00 PM

Put your slides in your team folder and fill in the "Our project" Doc. Then present for 4 minutes, with 1 minute for questions.

How to submit · How judging works

Checkpoints to aim for

  • First hour: a one-line problem, and who it helps. Talk to your mentor before you build anything.
  • Mid-morning: a plan for the smallest version that works.
  • Early afternoon: one feature working.
  • 15 minutes before 2:00: your slides in your team folder. Test every link in a private window.
  • At 2:00 PM: done. Stop changing the folder and practice your 4 minutes.

The 9 tracks

Every track uses the same five judging criteria.

Track 1 Growth & Infrastructure Capacity

How new homes and buildings put pressure on water, sewers, roads and other city services.

No ready-made challenge on this track. Pick any public data, say where it came from, and show who it helps. The City of Manvel's public maps are a good place to start. Public data ideas

Track 2 Drainage & Street Prioritization

No ready-made challenge on this track. Pick any public data, say where it came from, and show who it helps. The City of Manvel's public maps are a good place to start. Public data ideas
