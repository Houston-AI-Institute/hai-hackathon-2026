Questions

Help

On the day, look for the volunteers in white T-shirts. Before and after, email initiatives@houstonaiinstitute.com.

Full rules come with your team email on Wednesday 4 November.

Common questions

Can we work on more than one track?

Yes. When you hand in, pick one main track. That's the one the judges score.

We told our mentor a different track.

That's fine. Pick whichever track you're presenting.

We lost our team folder link.

Ask the help desk. Please don't post the link anywhere public: anyone with it can edit your folder.

Do we need to say which AI tools we used?

Yes, on one slide called "AI we used and how we checked it": what you used, and how you checked what it gave you.

How do we contact our mentor?

Your mentor is copied on your team email of Wednesday 4 November. Reply all to that email, so your whole team and the event team see it.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Slides, a mockup, a no-code tool or a clear plan all count. Good teams also need people who know the problem, can design, and can present.

Which tools can I use?

Any you like, as long as their terms allow your age. See the free tools table.

Under 18, and parents and guardians

On the day

  • A parent or guardian signs the student in at the desk at 8:30 and picks them up there at 4:30.
  • The on-site phone number is in our email to families.

Agreements

  • A parent or guardian must agree to the open-source rule. Reply to our email, or sign at the desk. Without it, the student can't take part.
  • The offshore incident reports challenge needs a parent's or guardian's yes. It's still the student's choice.
  • Photos are a separate yes or no answer from a parent or guardian.

Tools

Use only tools whose terms allow your age. Some, like Claude and Google AI Studio, are 18+ only. Each tool's rule is in the free tools table.

Google account

Use a personal Google account, not a school one. School accounts often can't open outside files.
